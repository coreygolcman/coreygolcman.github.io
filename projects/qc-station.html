Approach

Vision inspection

A Basler camera with dark-field, grazing-angle lighting makes edge rounding and embossed-lettering defects on the formed ABS sheets visible.

Defect detection model

An EfficientAD anomaly-detection model, trained with Anomalib, classifies each sheet as good or bad. It runs on an NVIDIA Jetson Orin AGX, where I set up the CUDA and Python environment for real-time inference at the edge.

[Add training data size, how you collected defect examples, and how you validated the model.]

Electrical integration

Relay-based circuits convert the Jetson's digital I/O to industrial I/O, and a voltage divider steps a 24 V industrial signal down to a level the Jetson can read. The station and the robotic arm coordinate through an automated handshake.

Fixturing and gripper

Parts are located using 3-2-1 principles and held with compliant clamping. A custom end-of-arm vacuum gripper separates one sheet at a time from a stack, and the robotic arm sorts each into a good or bad pile.
